Rural locations

Faroe tours are village tours: gorge hamlets, lagoon farms, and cliff settlements reached by boat or a single road.

  • Rural location

    Gjógv gorge village

    Gjógv · Half day · village and sea gorge

    Walk the turf-roof village of Gjógv and the natural harbour cut into the north coast of Eysturoy.

    Stay in Gjógv

  • Rural location

    Saksun lagoon

    Saksun · Half day · lagoon and turf roofs

    Follow the stream into Saksun, where turf-roof farms sit above the tidal lagoon on Streymoy’s quiet north shore.

    Stay in Saksun

  • Rural location

    Mykines cliff settlement

    Mykines · Full day · boat and lighthouse path

    Cross to the island village of Mykines and walk the puffin slopes toward the lighthouse, weather permitting.

    Stay on Mykines

  • Rural location

    Nólsoy island village

    Nólsoy · Half day · harbour village

    Ferry to the single village on Nólsoy and walk the island lane above the sound, facing Tórshavn from the rural side of the water.

    Stay on Nólsoy
