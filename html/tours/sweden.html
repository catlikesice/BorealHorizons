Rural locations

Sweden’s rural tours stay in the north and the mountains: national-park forest at Abisko, the Torne river village of Jukkasjärvi, and the slopes above Sälen.

  • Rural location

    Abisko national park walk

    Abisko · Half day · birch forest and the Kungsleden

    Walk from Abisko through birch forest toward the start of the Kungsleden, with Torneträsk and the open fell above the trees.

    Stays in Abisko

  • Rural location

    Jukkasjärvi river village

    Jukkasjärvi · Half day · riverbank and village

    Walk the Torne riverbank in Jukkasjärvi, the village north of Kiruna, past the wooden church and the ice-hotel meadows.

    Stay in Jukkasjärvi

  • Rural location

    Sälen mountain village

    Sälen · Half day · village trails

    Follow trails from the mountain village of Sälen into the forested slopes of the Transtrandsfjällen.

    Stays in Sälen
